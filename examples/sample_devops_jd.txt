# Senior / Staff Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) — Platform Infrastructure

## Company Overview
CandidateX Cloud delivers enterprise resilience and low-latency distributed edge compute. We are looking
for a Senior or Staff Site Reliability Engineer to design, scale, and automate our multi-region Kubernetes
infrastructure, GitOps pipelines, and observability frameworks.

## Responsibilities
- Architect and operate secure, highly available, multi-tenant Kubernetes clusters on AWS/GCP.
- Codify 100% of infrastructure provisioning using Terraform, Pulumi, and modular Infrastructure as Code.
- Implement end-to-end GitOps deployment pipelines with ArgoCD, Helm, and canary analysis tooling.
- Establish comprehensive observability with Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry across all production services.
- Define Service Level Objectives (SLOs), Service Level Indicators (SLIs), and error budgets with product engineering teams.
- Lead blameless incident postmortems, automate failure recovery, and run regular disaster recovery / chaos game days.

## Mandatory Requirements
- 5+ years in production Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, or Cloud Infrastructure roles.
- Expert-level proficiency with Kubernetes administration, networking, security policies, and debugging.
- Advanced mastery of Terraform / Infrastructure as Code and cloud architecture (AWS or GCP).
- Strong experience with production observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Datadog).
- Proficiency in at least one scripting/systems programming language (Python, Go, or Bash).

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with modern eBPF networking, service mesh (Cilium, Istio), or zero-trust network policies.
- Hands-on experience with chaos engineering tools (Chaos Mesh, Gremlin, Litmus).
- Contributions to open-source infrastructure or cloud-native tooling (CNCF ecosystem).
- Experience managing multi-region failover, active-active database clusters, and compliance (SOC2, HIPAA).
